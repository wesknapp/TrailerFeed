About & privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026

TrailerFeed is a free, non-commercial app for browsing new movie trailers. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What's stored, and where

Everything TrailerFeed remembers stays on your own device, in your browser's storage:

  • your filters, country and cinema choice
  • your saved list, watched list, and which trailers you've already seen
  • the categories you seem to like, which are used to order the feed
  • your own TMDB key, but only if you added one yourself

None of this is sent to us. To delete it, use Filters → Settings (reset recommendations / clear watch history), or clear this site's data in your browser.

What's sent to other services

  • Movie information: the app asks the TrailerFeed server for movie details. That server passes the request on to TMDB and keeps nothing about you. It runs on Cloudflare, which, like any web host, briefly processes your IP address to deliver the response and protect against abuse.
  • Trailers are played with YouTube's embedded player. When a trailer plays, YouTube and Google receive information as described in the Google Privacy Policy. By watching trailers you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).
  • Posters and images load from TMDB's image servers.
  • Links you choose to open (Find showtimes, Google Calendar, Watch on YouTube) take you to those sites, under their own privacy policies. The "Phone calendar" option creates a file on your device and sends nothing anywhere.

Credits

Movie data and images are provided by TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Streaming availability ("On Netflix" and so on) is provided by JustWatch.

Trailers are hosted on and played by YouTube. TrailerFeed isn't affiliated with YouTube, Google, TMDB, JustWatch or any cinema chain.
